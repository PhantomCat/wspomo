# Cookies & privacy

wspomo uses the minimum set of cookies needed for the app to work. No tracking, no advertising, no analytics IDs, no IP logging, no fingerprinting.

| Cookie | Purpose | Expires | HttpOnly |
|---|---|---|---|
| `wspomo_main` | Your timer settings (durations, workday schedule, sound, etc.) | 1 year | no |
| `wspomo_lang` | Interface language (ru / en) | 1 year | no |
| `wspomo_days` | Selected workdays | 1 year | no |
| `wspomo_theme` | Theme choice | 1 year | no |
| `wspomo_visitor` | **Random UUID** used only to count unique visitors and live users. Not linked to your identity, IP, or any personal data. | 1 year | yes |

The `wspomo_visitor` cookie contains a random UUID generated on the server. We store only: the UUID, first/last visit dates, and an anonymous "last seen" timestamp used for the «focusing now» counter. We never store your IP address, user-agent, or screen data. Aggregate public counters (visitors per day, focus minutes, pomodoros) are shown in the footer.

Deleting cookies removes all data associated with you on the client side; the anonymous counter aggregates remain as part of daily totals.
